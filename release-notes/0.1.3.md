# YT Musicfast 0.1.3

- **Music videos on your TV**: in Video mode, casting plays the music video full screen, with
  synced lyrics over it (the lyrics button turns them on or off)
- **TVs play through their own YouTube app**, so your Mac can sleep while the music goes on; turn
  it off per TV in the Connect panel. Your own files still stream from the Mac
- The next song is ready on the TV before the current one ends, and the progress bar shows the
  right length while casting
- **Play buttons react right away**: a spinner from the click, then pause while it plays,
  everywhere you can start music
- Quick skips never drop a press or play a song you skipped past
- About 60 MB less memory while playing
- Undo in the Edit menu, selecting several songs in the queue, clearer offline and error states
