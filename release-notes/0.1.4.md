# YT Musicfast 0.1.4

- **Casting to a TV is ad-free again**: the TV plays what your Mac streams. Playing through the TV's own
  YouTube app, so your Mac can sleep, is now something you turn on (Settings → Casting, or per TV from its
  ⋯ in the Connect panel). It plays without your account, so the TV may show ads, even when it's signed in
  to Premium
- **Lyrics on the TV** is in the ⚙︎ menu while you cast a music video, and in the TV's ⋯ in Connect; the
  lyrics button opens the lyrics on your Mac as usual
- A new Casting section in Settings
- Turning the TV's YouTube app on or off while casting no longer starts the video on your Mac
