# YT Musicfast 0.1.0

The first public release: a fast, native Mac app for YouTube Music.

- **Native and light** — SwiftUI and AVFoundation, no browser inside; about 150 MB of RAM and no CPU while idle
- **Better sound** — YouTube's Opus stream, or Premium's 256 kbps with a Premium account; equalizer, crossfade,
  gapless playback and volume normalization
- **Full-screen TV mode** (⇧⌘F) — a lean-back player made for a big screen, with lyrics, an Up next / Related
  rail and remote-style keys
- **Song/Video switch** that follows you from song to song, and music videos up to 4K
- **Downloads and offline**, Local Files, Your Stats, Listen together, Google Cast and AirPlay, a phone remote
- **Your account in step** — likes, playlists and History, with listening time reported as YouTube's own player
  does (can be turned off)
- **Keyboard first**, a menu-bar player, a miniplayer, a notch player, a command-line tool and Shortcuts actions

YT Musicfast is not affiliated with Google or YouTube. Builds aren't notarized yet: the first time, open it from
System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
